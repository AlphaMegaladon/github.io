## Who I am

I'm Martin, 33, from Germany. I work as an AI Solution Architect at a large consultancy with a focus on the EU.

How I got here? Partly, I blame sci-fi shows like Stargate and Stargate Atlantis. I always loved the scientists who figure things out. And I'm drawn to things I can't grasp at first sight. So I studied physics.

In my final year I worked with machine learning and AI for the first time. And it caught me again. So many possibilities. And of course sci-fi shows like Person of Interest. 😄 Years later, I'm still in it.

## What this blog is

A weekly log of things I've learned about these topics: 
- Work
- Private life 
- Finance 
- Whatever else is on my mind

## What to expect

One post every Friday. I give myself exactly one hour to write, then I hit publish, finished or not. So expect honest, sometimes rough notes rather than polished essays.

## Why I'm doing this

- Personal growth
- Reflecting on my week
- Structuring my notes
- Fleshing out some of my ideas
